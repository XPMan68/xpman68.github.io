Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026  ·  Developer: Greg Kie

ShiftClock is a shift and work-time tracking application for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is protected.

Data We Collect

ShiftClock stores only the data you enter directly into the app:

  • Shift records — start time, end time, and any notes you add
  • App settings and preferences you configure

The app does not collect your name, email address, device identifiers, location, or any other personal information.

Where Your Data Lives

All data is stored exclusively on your device using Android's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party — ever.

ShiftClock has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend infrastructure. Your shift records never leave your device.

Third-Party Services

ShiftClock does not integrate any advertising networks, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services, or social login providers. No data is shared with third parties.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions necessary for its core function. No permission is used to access data unrelated to work-time tracking.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstallation removes all locally stored records permanently.

Children

ShiftClock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
